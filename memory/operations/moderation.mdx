---
title: Moderation
description: How changes reach `main`.
---

No end-user submissions; the queue is PRs, RFCs, and template changes. Automation proposes, humans decide.

`proposed → checked → reviewed → merged`

Checked means CI green: `pnpm check`, `pnpm typecheck`, build, e2e where applicable, plus the Lefthook secret scan. Reviewed means human approval; Renovate owns dependency updates and security PRs merge anytime with 1 review. Merged means conventional commits via commitlint.

No merge on red. No bypass for bots. Outcomes live in the PR; spend lives in [Costs](./costs).
